05 / Process

How we'd work together.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We start with how the work gets done today: who does it, where it gets stuck, and what that costs you. An hour of listening saves weeks of building the wrong thing.

  2. 02

    Build

    I build in Laravel or Python and show you working pieces as I go. You see progress every step of the way, not one big reveal at the end.

  3. 03

    Launch

    I set up hosting and put it live. For websites I can keep looking after things afterwards, the way I do for Nyika Essence Safaris.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    You get written documentation, so your team understands the system and can keep working with it whether or not I'm around.
